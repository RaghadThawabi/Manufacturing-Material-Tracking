---
name: Manufacturing accounting
description: Business meaning and evidence boundaries for material tracking.
---
Reusable offcuts are not actual scrap, and recording them does not prove realized savings. Rework and unexplained differences must never silently become scrap.

**Why:** The user's challenge explicitly distinguishes these material outcomes.

**How to apply:** Preserve distinctions in validation, summaries, and future optimization. The two supplied jobs establish arithmetic examples only, not machine performance or root causes. Their shared machine does not establish comparability.

Management insights must identify the largest **recorded scrap reason**, not a proven root cause; do not invent causes or savings. Its share must use total recorded scrap in the same clearly defined filter scope and unit, distinguish approved from submitted results, and link to contributing production jobs.

**Why:** The user explicitly required traceable, actual-data insights while preserving existing dashboard functionality and company data.

**How to apply:** Keep illustrative samples opt-in for the new management insight. Do not mix units or silently treat awaiting-review records as approved evidence. Mark unknown comparison specifications rather than filling them in.

Pending rework is material still awaiting an outcome, not an additional issued quantity or a cumulative count of processing passes. Resolve it by transferring quantities to final outcomes while keeping issued material unchanged. Legacy records must not acquire invented specifications.

**Why:** Phase 2 must support rework without double counting and preserve existing factory records.

**How to apply:** Keep resolution atomic and version-checked. After a recorded transfer, prevent ordinary outcome editing from erasing its accounting history; reopen reviewed jobs before further transfers. Apply new mandatory specifications to new records, not retrospectively to the supplied samples.

Waste Detective's before/after evidence must exclude jobs on the action's calendar date and preserve the original investigated result as a snapshot.

**Why:** Production records only establish a date, not whether a job ran before or after an action that day. Later edits to production records must not silently rewrite the investigation's original evidence. Recorded improvement is not proof of causation.

**How to apply:** Use strictly later production dates for follow-up comparisons. Distinguish the flagged-job snapshot from the historical median used to detect it, and label live follow-up results as observed changes.

Keep Smart Cutting Advisor separate from existing production workflows and records. Rectangular layout planning should use deterministic geometric calculations, not AI-generated coordinates.

**Why:** The user explicitly prioritizes accurate calculations and a working simple solution without affecting existing pages, workflows, calculations or data. Planned leftover area is not measured scrap and may include reusable offcuts.

**How to apply:** Label proposals as estimates, not proven optima or machine-ready instructions. Do not turn unused area into kg waste, realized savings or production-accounting entries without separately established evidence.
