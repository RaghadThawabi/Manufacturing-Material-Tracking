description: Business meaning and evidence boundaries for material tracking.
---
Reusable offcuts are not actual scrap, and recording them does not prove realized savings. Rework and unexplained differences must never silently become scrap.

**Why:** The user's challenge explicitly distinguishes these material outcomes.

**How to apply:** Preserve distinctions in validation, summaries, and future optimization. The two supplied jobs establish arithmetic examples only, not machine performance or root causes. Their shared machine does not establish comparability.
