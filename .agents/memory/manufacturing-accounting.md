---
name: Manufacturing accounting
description: Business meaning and evidence boundaries for material tracking.
---
Reusable offcuts are not actual scrap, and recording them does not prove realized savings. Rework and unexplained differences must never silently become scrap.

**Why:** The user's challenge explicitly distinguishes these material outcomes.

**How to apply:** Preserve distinctions in validation, summaries, and future optimization. The two supplied jobs establish arithmetic examples only, not machine performance or root causes. Their shared machine does not establish comparability.

Pending rework is material still awaiting an outcome, not an additional issued quantity or a cumulative count of processing passes. Resolve it by transferring quantities to final outcomes while keeping issued material unchanged. Legacy records must not acquire invented specifications.

**Why:** Phase 2 must support rework without double counting and preserve existing factory records.

**How to apply:** Keep resolution atomic and version-checked. After a recorded transfer, prevent ordinary outcome editing from erasing its accounting history; reopen reviewed jobs before further transfers. Apply new mandatory specifications to new records, not retrospectively to the supplied samples.
